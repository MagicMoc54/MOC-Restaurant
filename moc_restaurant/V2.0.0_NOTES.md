# MOC Restaurant v2.0.0 - Business Management

Carries forward all v1.5.5 fixes.

Adds database support for:
- Restaurant employees
- Owner-created job ranks
- Per-rank pay
- Per-rank permission JSON
- Clocked-in state
- Minutes worked
- Payroll history
- Sales ledger

The banking adapter still needs to match the banking resource used on your server.
