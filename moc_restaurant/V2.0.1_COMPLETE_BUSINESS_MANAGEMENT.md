
# MOC Restaurant v2.0.1 - Complete Business Management

This replaces the earlier backend-only v2.0.

In-game Manager Station now provides:
- Clock in/out
- Employee list
- Hire online player by server ID
- Fire employees
- Change employee ranks
- Create owner-defined ranks
- Edit rank pay
- Edit rank permissions
- Delete unused non-owner ranks
- Payroll history
- Sales history

Admins can use `/mocrestaurant` -> Assign Restaurant Owner.

The Owner rank is protected and receives full business permissions.

Payroll:
- Runs for clocked-in online employees
- Attempts to withdraw from the restaurant job's qb-banking business account
- Deposits successful payroll into the employee bank
- Records failed payroll instead of generating money

Run `sql/upgrade_v2.0_to_v2.0.1.sql` before testing.
