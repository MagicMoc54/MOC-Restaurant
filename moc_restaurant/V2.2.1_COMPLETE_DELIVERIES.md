# MOC Restaurant v2.2.1 - Complete Deliveries & Restocking

This replaces the foundation-only v2.2 delivery milestone with a playable system.

## New placeable station
`Delivery Receiving`

Place it through:
`/mocrestaurant -> Open Builder -> Place Location -> Delivery Receiving`

Recommended interaction radius: `0.75`

## Business Management
Owners / authorized ranks now see:
`Deliveries & Restocking`

From there they can:
- View the wholesale ingredient catalog
- Order ingredients
- Choose quantities
- See delivery cost
- Track ordered / ready / delivered shipments

## Business-account purchasing
Supply orders are paid from the restaurant's qb-banking business account.

The account name uses the restaurant job name.

If:
- qb-banking is unavailable
- the account does not exist
- the restaurant cannot afford the shipment

the order is rejected instead of generating free supplies.

## Delivery timer
Default: 10 minutes.

Change:
`Config.Deliveries.DefaultLeadMinutes`

For testing, temporarily set it to `1`.

## Receiving the shipment
When the timer expires:
1. Go to the restaurant's `Delivery Receiving` station.
2. Press E.
3. Select the READY delivery.
4. Receive the entire shipment.
5. Move the ingredients from your inventory into the restaurant freezer/storage.

The server verifies that the player is physically near the correct restaurant's
Delivery Receiving station.

## Inventory safety
If the full shipment cannot be added, successfully added items are rolled back
and the delivery remains READY so it can be retried after making inventory space.

## Permissions
New rank permission:
`Manage Deliveries & Restocking`

Employees with Storage access can also receive ready deliveries while clocked in.

`sql/upgrade_v2.2_to_v2.2.1.sql`
