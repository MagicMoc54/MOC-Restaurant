# MOC Restaurant v2.2.6
## Automatic QBCore Job + qb-banking Business Account Setup

This update removes most of the manual setup required when creating a restaurant.

## Business Management

Two related options are available:

### Business Account / Job Setup
Set the internal restaurant job/account key.

Example:
`vanillaunicorn`

After saving it, MOC Restaurant immediately attempts to:
1. Create/update the runtime QBCore job.
2. Create the qb-banking job account if it does not exist.

### Automatic Business Setup
Shows:
- Job/account key
- Whether the QBCore job exists
- Whether the qb-banking account exists
- Current account balance when available

You can run the setup again safely.

## Duplicate safety

Existing QBCore jobs are reused/refreshed.
Existing qb-banking accounts are left alone and their balance is preserved.

A new business account starts with:
`Config.AutomaticBusinessSetup.StartingBusinessBalance`

Default: $0.

## Server startup

On resource startup, MOC Restaurant checks all restaurants that already have
a job/account key and attempts to ensure their QBCore job and qb-banking account
exist.

## Important limitation

QBCore jobs added at runtime are not the same thing as permanently editing
`qb-core/shared/jobs.lua`.

They remain available while the QBCore runtime supports them. MOC Restaurant
recreates its restaurant jobs at resource/server startup from its database ranks.

## qb-banking compatibility

This build uses:
- `GetAccountBalance`
- `CreateJobAccount`

If your qb-banking fork uses different export names, the setup screen will report
the failure instead of silently continuing.

No SQL update is required from v2.2.5 to v2.2.6.
