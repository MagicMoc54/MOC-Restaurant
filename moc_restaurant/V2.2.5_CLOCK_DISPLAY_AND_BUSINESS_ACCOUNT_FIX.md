# MOC Restaurant v2.2.5

Fixes two issues from v2.2.4.

## Clock In / Clock Out display

The database can return `clocked_in` as:
- true / false
- 1 / 0
- "1" / "0"

The client now normalizes all of those formats.

After clocking in:
- database state is saved
- Business Management refreshes
- the button displays `Clock Out`

After clocking out:
- the button returns to `Clock In`

## Business Account / Job Setup

Business Management now includes:
`Business Account / Job Setup`

Owners / authorized managers can set the restaurant's QBCore job and qb-banking
account key in-game.

Example:
`vanillaunicorn`

This saves to `moc_restaurants.job` and triggers the existing QBCore runtime
job synchronization.

Deliveries use this value to identify the restaurant business account.

## Important qb-banking note

The named business account must actually exist in your qb-banking setup.
Setting the value here tells MOC Restaurant which account/job to use; it does not
magically create a missing qb-banking account if your banking resource requires
separate account creation.

No SQL update is required from v2.2.4 to v2.2.5.
