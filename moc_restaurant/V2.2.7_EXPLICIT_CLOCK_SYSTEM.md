# MOC Restaurant v2.2.7 - Explicit Clock In / Clock Out

Clock In now explicitly requests `clocked_in = true`.
Clock Out now explicitly requests `clocked_in = false`.

After a successful server-side database update and verification:
- Clock In shows the notification "You are now clocked in."
- Business Management refreshes and shows Clock Out.
- Clock Out shows the notification "You are now clocked out."
- Business Management refreshes and shows Clock In.

Repeated clicks are safe and do not toggle to the wrong state.

No SQL update is required from v2.2.6 to v2.2.7.
