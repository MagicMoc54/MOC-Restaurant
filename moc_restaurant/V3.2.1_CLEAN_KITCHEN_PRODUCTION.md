# MOC Restaurant v3.2.1 - Clean Kitchen Production

This replaces the Ingredient Supplier branch.

## Removed
Ingredient Supplier is completely removed:
- no builder location
- no supplier prompt
- no supplier purchase menu
- no supplier server events

Restaurant bulk ingredients now come from:
Business Management -> Deliveries & Restocking -> Delivery Receiving

## New Kitchen Production System

Restaurant-specific recipes stored in `moc_restaurant_recipes` now drive the
actual kitchen stations.

Supported station types:
- Grill
- Fryer
- Prep Station
- Drink Station

Workflow:
1. Owner creates a restaurant-specific recipe in
   Business Management -> Menu Management.
2. Recipe is assigned to Grill/Fryer/Prep/Drink.
3. Clocked-in employee walks to that physical station.
4. Press E.
5. Only that restaurant's recipes for that station appear.
6. Script checks required ingredients.
7. Progress circle runs.
8. Ingredients are removed.
9. Finished food/drink is added to inventory.

Restaurants cannot see or craft another restaurant's recipes.

## Recipe example

Output Item:
`vu_burger`

Recipe Label:
`VU Burger`

Station:
`grill`

Ingredients:
`burger_bun:1, burger_patty:1`

## Required SQL

Run:
`sql/upgrade_v3.2_to_v3.2.1.sql`

This only removes obsolete placed `ingredient_supplier` locations.
It does not delete restaurant menu or recipe data.
